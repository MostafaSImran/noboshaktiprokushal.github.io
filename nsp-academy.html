Track 1 · Garments & IE

Industrial Engineering (IE), Garments & Apparel Management

For young industrial, mechanical and textile engineers, IE officers and production executives.

Master shop-floor efficiency, line balancing, and lean manufacturing to drive profitability in export-oriented RMG factories.

  • MOD-101 IE Techniques & Line Balancing Masterclass — work study, motion study, bottleneck identification, skill matrix tracking, target calculation
  • MOD-102 Lean Manufacturing & 5S Implementation — 7 wastes, SMED changeover, visual management systems
  • MOD-103 Apparel Merchandising & TNA Management — TNA calendars, costing, fabric booking, international buyer coordination
Track 2 · Textile & Sustainability

Textile Wet Processing, Chemical Safety & Higg FEM

For textile engineers, wet processing chemists, environmental officers and factory compliance managers.

Navigate global sustainability standards, chemical management, and environmental audits required by top-tier international brands.

  • MOD-201 Higg FEM 4.0 Compliance & Audit Preparation — environmental management systems, utility tracking, chemical management audits
  • MOD-202 ETP Operation & Industrial Wastewater Management — treatment processes, sludge management, pH optimization, dosing control
  • MOD-203 Advanced Wet Processing & Dyeing Mechanics — softflow, stenter, mercerizing, recipe management, right-first-time optimization
Track 3 · Utilities & Energy

Industrial Utilities, Boilers & Energy Management

For mechanical & electrical engineers, plant utility managers and senior maintenance technicians.

Optimize high-energy plant assets, ensure strict compliance with statutory boiler codes, and cut factory utility overhead.

  • MOD-301 Boiler Operations, Safety & Statutory Code Compliance — ASME/BNBC, combustion control, water treatment chemistry, blowdown management
  • MOD-302 Industrial Energy Audit & ISO 50001 Framework — steam trap auditing, compressed-air leak detection, chiller efficiency, payback calculations
  • MOD-303 HFO & Gas Utility Systems Management — HFO storage & pre-heating, burners, regulating & metering station (RMS) protocols
Track 4 · Safety & Compliance

Factory Safety, Fire Protection & Regulatory Design

For compliance officers, fire safety engineers, HR leads and factory managers.

Design, audit and maintain life-safety infrastructure compliant with NFPA, BNBC and statutory licensing (DGHS, Fire Service).

  • MOD-401 Industrial Fire Protection Design & Maintenance — NFPA 13, 14 & 101, sprinkler hydraulics, fire pump testing, evacuation planning
  • MOD-402 Bangladesh Labour Law & Social Compliance Audits — worker rights, safety committees, working hours, BSCI/SEDEX preparation
  • MOD-403 Medical Gas Pipeline Systems (NFPA-99 / HTM-2022) — design, installation, manifold operation and safety testing for healthcare facilities

Free introductory masterclasses stream regularly on our YouTube channel and Facebook page — join them first, then pick your track.
