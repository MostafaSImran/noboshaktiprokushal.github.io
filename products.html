NSP designs and fabricates complete vehicle body structures — engineered for strength, comfort and road conditions in Bangladesh. Our landmark achievement: the first luxury bus body in Bangladesh, built for Scania.

  • —Bus Body — luxury coach structures, structural framing and interior fit-out
  • —Caravan — customized travel and mobile living units
  • —Golf Cart — purpose-built utility and resort vehicles

Capsule Homes

Prefabricated capsule living units — factory-built, transportable and installable in days. A modern answer to resort housing, site accommodation and compact urban living, with integrated electromechanical systems.

  • —Factory fabrication with site installation in days, not months
  • —Integrated HVAC, electrical and plumbing modules
  • —Customizable layouts for resorts, staff housing and clinics
