Why an NSP Dryer

Clean, Gentle Heat — No Running Fuel Cost

Bangladesh grows mango, jackfruit, pineapple, banana, tomato, chilli and moringa in abundance — and loses a large share of every harvest to spoilage. Open sun-drying exposes food to dust, insects and road pollution. NSP dryers bring hospital-grade clean-air engineering to food drying.

01 · Clean Air

HEPA-Filtered Air Path

Three-stage G4 → F7 → H13 HEPA filtration and a sealed, positive-pressure air path keep dust, pollen, smoke and insects out of the product — even next to a busy road.

02 · Food Grade

SS304 Food-Contact Surfaces

Stainless-steel mesh trays, cabinet liner and air distributor — smooth, washable, corrosion-free and suitable for food-grade hygiene routines.

03 · Solar DC

100% Solar Power

150 Wp PV panel, MPPT controller and LiFePO4 battery run the fan and controls. No grid, no fuel, near-zero operating cost in solar mode.

04 · Indirect Drying

Better Colour & Nutrients

Food sits in an opaque cabinet, out of direct sunlight. Heated air does the work, so colour, aroma and light-sensitive vitamins are better preserved.

05 · Controlled Heat

50–60 °C Drying Air

Adjustable exhaust damper and fan speed hold the drying temperature in the ideal band — avoiding case-hardening and scorching.

06 · Hybrid Option

24-Hour Drying (M2)

A 1.5 kW PTC heater with safety interlock tops up on cloudy days and runs through the night — production that doesn't stop for the monsoon.

Technology

Clean Air In, Moisture Out

NSP dryers are indirect, fan-forced solar cabinet dryers. Outside air is filtered first, heated in a solar air collector, then pushed up through 12 stainless-steel trays and out of the chimney — carrying the moisture with it.

  • 1 · Filter

    Sealed filter box: G4 pre-filter, F7 fine filter, H13 HEPA.

  • 2 · Blow

    12 V DC EC blower pressurises the clean air path, so unfiltered air cannot leak in.

  • 3 · Heat

    Back-pass solar collector heats the air to about 50–60 °C. In M2, a PTC heater can take over.

  • 4 · Dry

    A perforated SS distributor spreads air evenly up through 12 SS304 trays.

  • 5 · Exhaust

    Moist air leaves via a damper, back-draft flap and rain-protected chimney.
